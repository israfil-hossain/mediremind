## 1. Introduction

Welcome to MediRemind. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our medicine reminder mobile application. By using MediRemind, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your email address, name (optional), and profile picture (optional) via Google Sign-In
- **Medication Data:** Names of medications, dosages, schedules, prescription details, and medication history
- **Health Data:** Information about your medications, reminders, and adherence patterns
- **Doctor-Patient Connections:** If using doctor-patient features, your connection to healthcare providers

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** App usage patterns, features used, and frequency of use via Firebase Analytics
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers
- **Log Data:** App crashes, performance issues, and technical diagnostic data

### 2.3 Third-Party Services
Our app integrates with the following services that may collect additional information:

| Service | Purpose | Data Collected |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Firebase Analytics | App analytics and crash reporting | Usage patterns, device info, crash logs |
| Firebase Authentication | User authentication | Email address, Google Sign-In data |
| Firebase Firestore | Cloud database | User data, medications, prescriptions |
| Stripe (via Stripe In-App Payments) | Payment processing for premium subscriptions | Payment details (processed securely by Stripe) |
| Google AdMob | Display ads | Advertising ID, usage patterns for ad targeting |
| Firebase Cloud Messaging | Push notifications | Device token for notifications |

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

1. **Core App Functionality**
   - Send medication reminders at scheduled times
   - Store and manage your medication list and prescriptions
   - Track your medication adherence history
   - Connect you with healthcare providers (if applicable)

2. **Service Improvement**
   - Analyze usage patterns to improve app performance
   - Fix bugs and crashes using crash reports
   - Develop new features based on user behavior

3. **Communication**
   - Send important app updates and notifications
   - Respond to your inquiries and support requests
   - Send promotional emails (you can opt-out)

4. **Subscription Management**
   - Process premium subscription payments via Stripe
   - Manage subscription status and billing

5. **Advertising**
   - Display relevant ads for free-tier users
   - Personalize ad experience (without revealing sensitive health data)

## 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We **do not sell** your personal information. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

### 4.1 Service Providers
- **Firebase (Google):** For backend services, authentication, database, and analytics
- **Stripe:** For secure payment processing (we do not store your payment details)
- **Google AdMob:** For ad serving (uses advertising ID, not personal health data)

### 4.2 Doctor-Patient Feature
If you use the doctor-patient connection feature:
- Your medication data may be shared with healthcare providers you explicitly connect with
- Doctors can view your medication adherence and prescriptions
- You can revoke doctor access at any time

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests.

## 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
- **Firebase Security:** Firebase provides authentication and authorization
- **Secure Authentication:** OAuth 2.0 via Google Sign-In
- **Payment Security:** Stripe PCI DSS compliant payment processing
- **Access Controls:** Limited access to user data by authorized personnel only

## 6. Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** Data is retained while your account is active
- **Deleted Accounts:** Data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion
- **Anonymous Analytics:** Some anonymized analytics data may be retained indefinitely

## 7. Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

| Right | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Access** | View all data stored in your account via the app |
| **Deletion** | Delete your account and all associated data |
| **Correction** | Update or correct your medication information |
| **Opt-out** | Disable analytics, unsubscribe from marketing emails |
| **Revoke Doctor Access** | Remove doctor-patient connections at any time |

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@flowentech.com

## 8. Children's Privacy

## 9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. Firebase servers are located globally. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place per GDPR requirements.
